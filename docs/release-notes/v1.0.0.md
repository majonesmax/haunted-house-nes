The first release of *Haunted House: By Matchlight*: Atari 2600 *Haunted House*, rule for rule, as a native
NES game, in a new mansion - the Cellar, the Foyer, the Gallery, the Bedrooms and the Attic.

- **Download** `haunted_house.nes` and open it in any NES emulator (it's an MMC3 cartridge: 64K of program,
  64K of tiles). `SHA256SUMS.txt` has its checksum.
- **Controls:** the D-pad walks; A lights a match and puts down what you hold; Start starts a game; Select
  chooses the game (1-9); B gives a hint in play and the help on the title; B + Select is the lights-on
  cheat.
- **The same game as the original:** the engine is checked frame by frame against the remaster's model of
  the original, on this mansion - every frame exact, none late.
- **Made from text:** the mansion's plans, its art and its songs are text in the repository; nothing from
  the 2600 game is in it or in the ROM.
- **The original's own mansion:** build it yourself from your copy of the 2600 game -
  [docs/faithful-build.md](https://github.com/majonesmax/haunted-house-nes/blob/main/docs/faithful-build.md)
  has the recipe.
