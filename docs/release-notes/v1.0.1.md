Three small fixes to *Haunted House: By Matchlight*. The same ROM layout as v1.0.0 (an MMC3 cartridge);
`SHA256SUMS.txt` has its checksum.

- **The front door is on the Foyer.** It was drawn on the Cellar's wall, a floor below, where it blended
  into the brickwork; the Foyer, the floor with the way out, had none. It's a wood door at the right-hand
  edge of the Foyer's middle row now, seen by matchlight and with the lights-on cheat.
- **The story is told over the dark.** It had the title's blue sky and its lightning, and with no mansion in
  sight the flashes looked like a fault.
- **Thunder on the title.** A roll of thunder follows each flash of lightning on the title, game over and
  the help, a second later. In play the lightning stays what it was in the original: a warning that a
  monster is in the room.
