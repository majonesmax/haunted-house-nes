An ambient ROM for *Haunted House: By Matchlight*, beside the usual one. `SHA256SUMS.txt` has both
checksums.

- **`haunted_house_ambient.nes`:** the same game, with no tunes on the floors. In their place, the house: a
  low hum that comes and goes, wind, a music box somewhere far off, sighs, knocking, footsteps on the floor
  above - and, once in a while, after a long hush, something worse. The title, the story, the danger music
  and the win are as they were. Made for playing in the dark, on an old TV.
- **`haunted_house.nes`:** unchanged in play; each floor keeps its song.
- **The browser page** has a choice of the two, and remembers which you played last:
  https://majonesmax.github.io/haunted-house-nes/
