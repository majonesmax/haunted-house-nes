Two fixes from a player's notes, in both ROMs (`haunted_house.nes` and `haunted_house_ambient.nes`).
`SHA256SUMS.txt` has their checksums.

- **The match's hand stays by you.** Walking to the far left of a narrow corridor with a match lit, the hand
  holding it jumped to the middle of the screen. With no room on the left, the match is held on the right now.
- **The scepter is a golden staff.** Its head had a gold piece hanging off one side, and it read as an axe.
  Now it's a gold point, a red jewelled orb and a collar on a straight staff. What touches it is what you
  see, so its outline for picking it up follows the new picture.

The browser page plays both: https://majonesmax.github.io/haunted-house-nes/
